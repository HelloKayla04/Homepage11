KEROPPI

TOP FIVE FACTS

  • He is known for his large eyes and V-shaped mouth.
  • Keroppi is an energetic, green-skinned frog character created by Sanrio in 1988
  • He lives at Donut Pond with his family, including his triplet siblings Pikki and Koroppi.
  • His birthday is July 10th.
  • He is a popular, optimistic character who enjoys swimming, baseball, and adventures.
