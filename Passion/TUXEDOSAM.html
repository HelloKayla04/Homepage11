TUXEDOSAM

TOP FIVE FACTS

  • Tuxedosam is a popular Sanrio character debuted in 1979.
  • He is a plump, blue Emperor penguin from Tuxedo Island in Antarctica who wears a sailor suit, a red bow tie, and a round cap.
  • He's also a Navy veteran. He holds a second officer's license and has served in eight Navy battles..
  • Known for being fashionable and a bit clumsy, he owns 365 bow ties and speaks fluent English after studying abroad in England.
  • He was intended to be the boyfriend of Sanrio's mascot. Ironically, his dream is to fall in love with Kitty.
