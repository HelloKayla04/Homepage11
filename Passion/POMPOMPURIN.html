POMPOMPURIN

TOP FIVE FACTS

  • Pompompurin is a beloved Sanrio golden retriever character created in 1996.
  • His name represents both his brown beret ("pom pom") and the Japanese word for custard pudding ("purin").
  • Purin was born on a sunny day on April 16th.
  • Pompompurin's best friend is Muffin, a small, smart brown-and-white hamster who enjoys hiding in Pompompurin's beret.
  • The popular Sanrio Golden Retriever, is frequently nicknamed Purin or Pompom. Because of his resemblance to the dessert, he is often called the Pudding Dog.
