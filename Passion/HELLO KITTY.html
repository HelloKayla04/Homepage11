HELLO KITTY

TOP FIVE FACTS

  • Hello Kitty, created by Yuko Shimizu for Sanrio in 1974, is actually a British schoolgirl named Kitty White.
  • She is described as being 5 apples tall and weighing 3 apples.
  • She ran for president of the United States in 2016 on the Friendship Party.
  • Hello Kitty has collaborated with countless brands. Recent ones include Adidas, McDonald’s, Crocs, Gund, Forever 21 and Glamnetic Nails.
