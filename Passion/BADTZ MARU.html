TOP FIVE FACTS

  • Badtz-Maru is one of the main characters in the show.
  • He owns an arcade named "XO Arcade" and is himself an experienced gamer.
  • Badtz-Maru's favorite food is high-priced sushi, specifically enjoyed in Ginza. He is also famously fond of Poripari Ramen.
  • Badtz-Maru was first introduced in 1993. His birthday is on April Fools' Day, which fits his playful personality!
  • He attends the first grade at the Gorgeous Academy.
